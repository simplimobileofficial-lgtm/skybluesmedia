OUR STORY

About Us

Independent coverage. One Sky Blue community.

The Home of the Sky Blues.

Sky Blues Media is an independent Coventry City football media platform built around a shared passion for the club, its history and its supporters.


We bring the Sky Blue community closer to the action through news, transfer updates, match previews, matchday coverage, results and features. Our aim is to deliver engaging, reliable and professional football coverage while celebrating everything that makes Coventry City special.


From the stands at the CBS Arena to supporters around the world, we are here to connect fans and share the stories that matter.
